Fishing Terms in Quapaw

Posted September 8th, 2026

Earlier this summer, our marketing department asked us to produce some terms for the fishing season. We are posting them here so our blog readers can enjoy them as well! There is also a printable document linked at the bottom of this post.

General Fishing Terms

Quapaw Term English Meaning
ho fish
ni water
watʰíška creek, river
ni sákki water rapids, current in moving water
ho wébaxto fish spear
ho wékixe fishing net
ho wébitti fishing weir

Types of Fish

Quapaw Term English Meaning
ho bdóka sucker fish
ho tté hóšoka trout
ho ppási stétte gar fish
ho dátte freshwater drum fish
ho ttáma dogfish (bowfin)
ho stétte eel
ppasí odokáhi spoonbill
ho ką́ni buffalo fish (ictiobus)
mixé Black Bullhead catfish
mixé ha sáki white catfish
mižą́į Yellow Bullhead catfish

Parts of a Fish

Quapaw Term English Meaning
ho šókka fish bladder
ho ppízi fish gall bladder
ho ppí fish liver
ho šénąkke fish scales
ho ištá fish eyes
ho íbe tail fin
